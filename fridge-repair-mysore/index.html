Fridge Problems We Fix

Tell us what your appliance is doing. Our team can help identify the next step.

Fridge Not Cooling

Low gas, a faulty compressor or relay, failed thermostat or dirty condenser coil can affect cooling.

Ask about this repair →
✦

Freezer Works but Fridge Is Warm

A defrost issue or failed fan may prevent cold air from reaching the lower compartment.

Ask about this repair →
✦

Too Much Ice or Frost

A faulty defrost heater, timer or sensor, or a poor door seal can cause frost build-up.

Ask about this repair →
✦

Water Leaking Inside or Under Fridge

A blocked defrost drain or damaged drain tray may cause water to collect.

Ask about this repair →
✦

Noisy Fridge or Clicking Compressor

A worn fan motor, failing compressor relay or loose component can cause unusual sounds.

Fridge Not Turning On or Tripping

Power, PCB, relay or wiring faults may be responsible. The power supply and stabiliser can also be checked.

Door Not Closing or Sealing

A loose or torn door gasket lets warm air in and can make the compressor work harder.

Fridge Repair: Frequently Asked Questions

How much does fridge repair cost in Mysore?

Fridge repairs start from ₹300. The visit and inspection are free. The final cost depends on the fault and any spare part required.

Why is my fridge not cooling?

Possible causes include low gas from a leak, a faulty compressor relay or thermostat, a failed fan, a blocked defrost system or a dirty condenser coil.

How much does fridge gas refilling cost?

It depends on fridge type, refrigerant and the location of any leak. The team checks the fault and shares a quote before work.

Is it worth repairing my old fridge?

The technician can explain the likely repair and cost so you can compare repair with replacement before deciding.

Do you repair double-door, side-by-side and inverter fridges?

Yes. The service brief lists single-door, double-door, side-by-side, frost-free, direct-cool and inverter refrigerators.

Will you repair the fridge at my home?

Most fridge repairs are carried out at home. If a part or additional work affects timing, the team will explain before proceeding.

Can a technician come today?

In most parts of Mysore, we aim to reach within 30 minutes during working hours. Call or WhatsApp to confirm.

Is there a warranty on fridge repair?
